Exploring the fundamentally social nature of the human mind

Understanding human sociality through behavior, cognition, and computation

Humans are exceptionally sensitive to the welfare of others and to the social states around them. This social sensitivity has both constructive expressions—such as empathy and altruism—and darker ones, including envy, prejudice, and discrimination. We seek to understand the evolutionary and socio-ecological foundations of this sociality and how it is implemented in the human mind.

Our work connects insights from the humanities and social sciences with empirical findings and computational approaches in behavioral science, cognitive science, neuroscience, economics, evolutionary biology, informatics, philosophy of law, and ethics.

Since 2024

Based at Meiji Gakuin University, the group provides a meeting ground for open discussion and collaboration among researchers across institutions and disciplines.
